Nest for Mac — first launch on macOS
=====================================

If macOS says “Nest” Not Opened or “could not verify”:

OPTION A (easiest)
1. Drag Nest.app to Applications (if you have not already).
2. Double-click “Open Nest (First Time).command” in this window.
   It clears the download quarantine and opens Nest once.

OPTION B (Finder)
1. Open Applications in Finder.
2. Right-click (or Control-click) Nest → Open.
3. Click Open in the dialog. You only do this once.

OPTION C (System Settings)
1. Try opening Nest once (you may see the warning).
2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Nest.

Nest is not notarized yet because Apple charges for the Developer certificate
required to pass Gatekeeper automatically. The app runs entirely on your Mac.
